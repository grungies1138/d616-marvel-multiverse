# Wickfield Eight — Rank 2 Pregenerated Characters
### Marvel Multiverse Role-Playing Game · One-Shot Ready

---

## Before You Play

**These are eight original, Rank 2 characters** built for a street-level "classic team-up" one-shot. All eight use the same Rank so no one out-shines anyone else at the table.

**A note on precision:** Abilities, Defenses, Health, Focus, Karma, and Speed all follow the Core Rulebook's actual formulas (Ability Points = 5 × Rank, max score = Rank + 3, Defense = 10 + Ability, Health = Resilience × 30, Focus = Vigilance × 30, Karma = Rank). Power Sets and named Powers below are drawn from the real system (Super-Strength, Martial Arts, Ranged Weapons, Elemental Control, Power Control, etc.), but the exact Focus costs and damage numbers are streamlined for pick-up-and-play speed rather than copied verbatim from the book. If you own the Core Rulebook and want to swap in the official text for any named power, that'll drop in cleanly — nothing here contradicts it, it's just simplified.

**Dice refresher:** Roll 2 white d6 + 1 red Marvel Die (dM), add the relevant Ability score, compare to the target's Defense (or a flat difficulty). If the dM shows the Marvel symbol (counts as 6), the check is **Fantastic** — you get a bonus effect (noted on select powers below). If the dM shows a 6, the result is **Green** — even a hit comes with a complication the GM introduces. **Edge** = reroll one die, keep the better total. **Trouble** = reroll one die, keep the worse total. Karma can be spent to buy an Edge or to fuel a recovery roll.

**Turn structure:** one Move + one Standard Action + one Reaction (between turns), plus whatever a trait or power adds.

---

## The Hook (optional, swap for your own)

All eight have a loose tie to the **Wickfield Community Center**: Amberlight volunteers there, Bulwark's little cousin is in the after-school program, Circuit rewired its ancient WiFi for free, The Latch works security for the block, and the rest have all passed through its doors at some point. The week of the fundraiser, the block starts losing power in rolling waves — not a grid failure, but something *pulling* ambient bioelectric and kinetic energy out of the neighborhood, block by block, moving toward the Center itself. Whatever's doing it will be at full strength by the night of the fundraiser. That's your one-shot.

# 1. BULWARK
**Marcus "Mo" Odom · Rank 2 · Mutant · Dockworker**

*The guy who's been strong his whole life and spent just as long being careful about it. Slow to start a fight, immovable once he's in one.*

| Ability | Score | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| Melee | +4 | 14 |
| Agility | 0 | 10 |
| Resilience | +4 | 14 |
| Vigilance | +1 | 11 |
| Ego | 0 | 10 |
| Logic | +1 | 11 |

**Health** 120 · **Focus** 30 · **Karma** 2 · **Speed** 5 · **Initiative** +1

**Traits**
- *Ironhide (Occupation):* Edge on Melee checks to lift, carry, or break heavy objects.
- *Unbreakable Will:* Edge to resist Fear, Intimidation, or mind-control effects (Ego Defense checks).
- *Slow to Anger:* Trouble on your first attack roll each scene. Once you land a hit, gain Edge on all further Melee attacks for the rest of the scene. (GM: award 1 Karma the first time this costs him something.)

**Power Set — Super-Strength**
- **Mighty 1** — Standard Action, melee. Melee attack vs. target's Resilience Defense. Hit: 15 damage, target knocked back 1 square. *Cost: 0 Focus.*
- **Mighty 2** *(requires Mighty 1)* — Standard Action, melee, two-handed haymaker. Melee attack vs. Resilience Defense. Hit: 25 damage; if the target is Small or Normal size, they're also knocked prone. **Fantastic:** target is thrown back 3 squares, taking +10 damage if they hit terrain or another creature. *Cost: 10 Focus.*
- **Sturdy 1** — Passive. Damage Reduction 5 against physical attacks, always on.
- **Banging Heads** — Standard Action, melee, requires two adjacent enemies. Melee attack vs. both targets' Resilience Defense. Hit: 12 damage to each. *Cost: 10 Focus.*

*Synergy:* Best up front. Let Mo eat the alpha strike and hold the chokepoint while Ricochet or Circuit pick people off behind him.

## Running Them at the Table

- **Handing out sheets:** if your group leans tactical, Bulwark, Circuit, and Permafrost reward planning; Ricochet, Wisp, and The Latch reward fast, reactive players; Nightglass and Amberlight are the two with the most narrative/utility swing.
- **Team Maneuvers:** the official character sheet has a slot for these — if your table wants one, a clean pregen combo is Permafrost slowing a target with Elemental Blast/Burst, then Wisp or Ricochet moving in on the reduced Speed to finish it off. Treat it as GM-adjudicated Edge rather than a hard rule unless you're cross-referencing the Core Rulebook's actual Team Maneuver text.
- **Karma:** everyone starts each session with Karma equal to Rank (2). Traits marked "GM: award Karma" are your cue to hand out more when a player leans into a flaw instead of around it.
