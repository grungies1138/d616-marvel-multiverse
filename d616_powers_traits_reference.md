# D616 Compendium — Full Powers, Gear & Traits Reference

Every Power and Common Weapon from the Marvel Multiverse RPG Core Rule Book (facts exact, wording original, see the system README for the full sourcing note), the book's guidance on writing Traits (which, unlike Powers and Gear, aren't a fixed book catalog — see Part 3), every custom Power and Gear item in the **Wickfield Eight Items (Homebrew)** pack, every Trait available across the eight Wickfield Eight pregens plus their adversaries, and the one-shot's villain and henchman template from **Wickfield Eight: Adversaries**. This is the source content compiled into the in-Foundry **D616 Reference** journal compendium and the sheet's hover tooltips — see README. (The book-sourced Powers and Common Weapons previously also shipped as standalone Item compendiums; those were removed in 1.5.3 since this document and the Reference journal already cover the same content — the actual usable Item versions of Powers/Gear live in the Homebrew pack and on the pregens/adversaries.)

**Totals:** 307 book-sourced Powers + 12 book-sourced Common Weapons (Gear) + 24 custom hero Powers + 6 custom hero Gear + 5 adversary Powers = **336 Powers**, **18 Gear items**, and **29 Traits**.

## Part 1 — Powers (Reference): Core Rulebook (307)

### Basic / Standalone Powers (40)

- **Accuracy 1** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on: +1 Damage Multiplier, +1 non-attack check bonus to Agility.)* Sharpens the character's aim, adding +1 to their Agility damage multiplier and giving a +1 bonus on non-attack Agility checks.
- **Accuracy 2** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Accuracy 1, Rank 2. *(Passive, always on: +2 Damage Multiplier, +2 non-attack check bonus to Agility.)* Further hones the character's precision, adding +2 to their Agility damage multiplier and a +2 bonus on non-attack Agility checks.
- **Accuracy 3** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Accuracy 2, Rank 3. *(Passive, always on: +3 Damage Multiplier, +3 non-attack check bonus to Agility.)* Refines the character's marksmanship further, adding +3 to their Agility damage multiplier and a +3 bonus on non-attack Agility checks.
- **Accuracy 4** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Accuracy 3, Rank 4. *(Passive, always on: +4 Damage Multiplier, +4 non-attack check bonus to Agility.)* Perfects the character's targeting, adding +4 to their Agility damage multiplier and a +4 bonus on non-attack Agility checks.
- **Additional Limbs** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* Grants one extra limb, or a matched pair, usable like a hand for holding items and taking actions, giving an edge on Melee and Agility checks that benefit from the extra reach.
- **Anger** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 10 Focus (scales: +1 Damage Modifier per 10 extra Focus, min 10 to activate)*. Prerequisites: Mighty 3, Rank 4. After being hurt, the character flies into a rage: for every 10 Focus spent they gain +15 to their Melee damage bonus but take -2 to Logic (and Logic defense) for as long as they concentrate, and cannot use other concentration powers meanwhile.
- **Brawling** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* Lets the character use their Melee defense score in place of their Agility defense when defending against Agility-based attacks.
- **Combat Trickery** — *Reaction, Instant, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Rank 2. Once per battle, when attacking a foe (or foes) of equal or higher rank, the character automatically gets the best Marvel die result on that attack, unaffected by trouble.
- **Discipline 1** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on: +1 Damage Multiplier, +1 non-attack check bonus to Ego.)* A permanent training bonus: +1 to the character's Ego damage multiplier and +1 on Ego checks that aren't attacks.
- **Discipline 2** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Discipline 1, Rank 2. *(Passive, always on: +2 Damage Multiplier, +2 non-attack check bonus to Ego.)* Deeper mastery of the character's power: +2 to their Ego damage multiplier and +2 on Ego checks that aren't attacks.
- **Discipline 3** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Discipline 2, Rank 3. *(Passive, always on: +3 Damage Multiplier, +3 non-attack check bonus to Ego.)* Near-total command of the character's power: +3 to their Ego damage multiplier and +3 on Ego checks that aren't attacks.
- **Discipline 4** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Discipline 3, Rank 4. *(Passive, always on: +4 Damage Multiplier, +4 non-attack check bonus to Ego.)* Total mastery of the character's power: +4 to their Ego damage multiplier and +4 on Ego checks that aren't attacks.
- **Disguise** — *Standard Action, Permanent, Melee, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* With a standard action, the character reshapes their appearance to look like someone else; onlookers must beat the character's Ego defense to see through it, with an edge if they personally know the person being impersonated.
- **Energy Absorption** — *Reaction, Instant, 15 Focus*. Prerequisites: Rank 4. When the character takes Health damage, they can convert it into Focus instead, gaining up to double their normal Focus maximum; the power can't be used again until that bonus Focus is spent, and any leftover fades once combat ends.
- **Environmental Protection** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character is immune to extreme cold, extreme heat, crushing deep-sea pressure, radiation, and vacuum, choosing which of these apply to fit their concept.
- **Evasion** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character may use their Agility defense score instead of their Melee defense when defending against melee attacks.
- **Flight 1** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Rank 2. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character gains flight, with a combat Flight Speed equal to their rank times their Run Speed, and can travel three times that speed outside combat.
- **Flight 2** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Flight 1, Rank 3. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* Outside combat, the character's flight speed jumps to fifty times their Flight Speed, far exceeding Flight 1's travel rate.
- **Group Flight** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Flight 1, Rank 2. For every point of Ego, the character can carry one ally aloft with them, moving as a group at half the character's Flight Speed while everyone stays within 5 spaces times rank; the character can release any ally at will.
- **Healing Factor** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* At the end of each of the character's turns, they automatically regain Health equal to their Resilience, in or out of combat.
- **Heightened Senses 1** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character can perceive things roughly twice as far as normal, gains an edge on Vigilance checks to notice things, and enemies have trouble sneaking past them.
- **Heightened Senses 2** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Heightened Senses 1. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character's senses now reach roughly four times normal range and can compensate for a lost or blocked sense, even letting them judge honesty by listening to a heartbeat; they gain a double edge on Vigilance checks to notice things and enemies have double trouble sneaking past them.
- **Iconic Weapon** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character is renowned for a unique, signature weapon, subject to the Narrator's approval of its details; this power can be taken again for each additional distinct weapon they become known for.
- **Inspiration** — *Standard Action, 1 round, 0 Focus*. The character rallies an ally within earshot, granting them an edge on all action checks until the start of the character's next turn.
- **Integrity** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character may use their Logic defense score in place of their Ego defense when defending against Ego attacks.
- **Mighty 1** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on: +1 Damage Multiplier, +1 non-attack check bonus to Melee.)* Makes the character count as one size larger for lifting, carrying, swinging, and throwing, and grants a bonus to their Melee damage multiplier and to non-attack Melee checks.
- **Mighty 2** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Mighty 1, Rank 2. *(Passive, always on: +2 Damage Multiplier, +2 non-attack check bonus to Melee.)* Makes the character count as two sizes larger for lifting, carrying, swinging, and throwing, with a larger bonus to their Melee damage multiplier and non-attack Melee checks.
- **Mighty 3** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Mighty 2, Rank 3. *(Passive, always on: +3 Damage Multiplier, +3 non-attack check bonus to Melee.)* Makes the character count as three sizes larger for lifting, carrying, swinging, and throwing, with a further bonus to their Melee damage multiplier and non-attack Melee checks.
- **Mighty 4** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Mighty 3, Rank 4. *(Passive, always on: +4 Damage Multiplier, +4 non-attack check bonus to Melee.)* Makes the character count as four sizes larger for lifting, carrying, swinging, and throwing, with the largest bonus to their Melee damage multiplier and non-attack Melee checks.
- **Reinforced Skeleton** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on: +1 Health Damage Reduction.)* Bones reinforced with or replaced by super-strong alloy make the character innately tougher, reducing all incoming Health damage by 1.
- **Shape-Shift** — *Standard Action, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Disguise, Rank 3. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character can transform into another shape up to one size larger or smaller than normal, gaining that form's natural traits (such as flight as a bird, or water-breathing as a fish) while transformed.
- **Slow-Motion Dodge** — *Reaction, Instant, 0 Focus*. As a reaction when an enemy attacks their Agility defense, time seems to crawl for the character, giving the attacker trouble on the roll.
- **Sturdy 1** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on: +1 Health Damage Reduction.)* The character's body naturally shrugs off some physical harm, granting 1 point of Health Damage Reduction. If the toughness comes from removable gear rather than the body itself, the Tech Reliance trait should apply to it.
- **Sturdy 2** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sturdy 1, Rank 2. *(Passive, always on: +2 Health Damage Reduction.)* The character's resilience improves further, granting 2 points of Health Damage Reduction.
- **Sturdy 3** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sturdy 2, Rank 4. *(Passive, always on: +3 Health Damage Reduction.)* The character's durability grows again, granting 3 points of Health Damage Reduction.
- **Sturdy 4** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sturdy 3, Rank 6. *(Passive, always on: +4 Health Damage Reduction.)* At its peak, the character's toughness grants 4 points of Health Damage Reduction.
- **Uncanny 1** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* Cuts the damage multiplier of any attack targeting the character's Focus by 1.
- **Uncanny 2** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Uncanny 1, Rank 2. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* Cuts the damage multiplier of any attack targeting the character's Focus by 2.
- **Uncanny 3** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Uncanny 2, Rank 4. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* Cuts the damage multiplier of any attack targeting the character's Focus by 3.
- **Uncanny 4** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Uncanny 3, Rank 6. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* Cuts the damage multiplier of any attack targeting the character's Focus by 4.

### Elemental Control (17)

- **Elemental Barrage** — *Standard Action, Instant, 10 spaces, 15 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Blast, Rank 4. *(Ego check vs. Resilience Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: affected targets take full damage instead of half, plus the element's special effect.)* The character makes one Ego check against the Resilience defense of every enemy within a 10-space burst, dealing half damage to each affected target on success.
- **Elemental Barrier** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 10 spaces per rank, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Blast, Rank 2. *(Agility check vs. Agility Defense; Fantastic: the target also suffers the element's special effect.)* The character raises a wall of their element at range, making an Agility check against anyone caught in its path to decide which side of the wall they end up on; the wall absorbs up to 10 damage per hit before breaking, keeping people behind it unharmed either way.
- **Elemental Blast** — *Standard Action, Instant, 10 spaces, 5 Focus (scales: +1 Damage Modifier per 2 extra Focus, min 5 to activate)*. Prerequisites: Elemental Burst, Rank 2. *(Agility check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: double damage and the element's special effect.)* The character makes a ranged attack with an edge, adding +1 to their Agility damage bonus for every 2 Focus spent.
- **Elemental Burst** — *Standard Action, Instant, 10 spaces, 0 Focus*. *(Agility check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: double damage and the element's special effect.)* The character makes a basic ranged elemental attack against a foe in sight, dealing normal damage on a success.
- **Elemental Form** — *Passive, Permanent, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Reinforcement, Rank 3. *(Passive, always on — see Effect.)* The character's body is permanently made of their element, giving them unlimited access to it; if reduced to 0 Health the body loses cohesion and falls apart, but can re-form once Health returns.
- **Elemental Grab** — *Standard Action, Concentration, Varies, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Burst, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Melee Defense; Fantastic: the target is also pinned and suffers the element's special effect.)* The character makes an Ego attack against a target's Melee defense within range to snare them with their element; breaking free requires a Melee check against target number 20.
- **Elemental Infusion** — *Standard Action, Concentration, Melee, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Burst, Rank 2. The character channels their elemental energy into a weapon they're holding; while infused, a Fantastic success attacking with that weapon also triggers the element's special effect.
- **Elemental Prison** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 spaces times the character's rank, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Protection 1. *(Ego check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: trapped targets take full damage plus the element's special effect.)* The character encloses chosen targets in a cage of their element, making an Ego check against each target's Agility defense to trap them inside; the cage absorbs incoming damage the same way the character's Elemental Protection does, breaking if it takes more than that but leaving anyone inside unharmed.
- **Elemental Protection 1** — *Reaction, Concentration, Melee, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Barrier, Rank 2. When attacked or endangered, the character raises an elemental shield that instantly absorbs up to 10 damage per hit; anything beyond that breaks the shield and lets the excess through.
- **Elemental Protection 2** — *Reaction, Concentration, Melee, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Protection 1, Rank 3. A sturdier elemental shield: it instantly absorbs up to 20 damage per hit before breaking and letting the excess through.
- **Elemental Protection 3** — *Reaction, Concentration, Melee, 15 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Protection 2, Rank 4. A heavily reinforced elemental shield: it instantly absorbs up to 30 damage per hit before breaking and letting the excess through.
- **Elemental Protection 4** — *Reaction, Concentration, Melee, 20 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Protection 3, Rank 5. The character's mightiest elemental shield: it instantly absorbs up to 40 damage per hit before breaking and letting the excess through.
- **Elemental Push** — *Standard Action, Instant, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Burst, Rank 3. *(Ego check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: the target also takes regular damage, is knocked prone, and suffers the element's special effect.)* The character makes an Ego attack against a target's Agility defense to shove them with their element up to 1 space per rank in any direction.
- **Elemental Reinforcement** — *Reaction, Instant, Melee, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Protection 1. When damage leaks through one of the character's elemental damage-reduction powers, they can redirect that damage to their Focus instead of their Health, keeping the protection intact.
- **Elemental Ricochet** — *Standard Action, Instant, 10 spaces times the character's rank, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Burst, Rank 3. *(Agility check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: the blast ricochets to a new target in sight, allowing another attack roll, chaining further while each hit stays fantastic.)* The character makes a ranged elemental attack against a target in sight, dealing regular damage on a success.
- **Elemental Suffocation** — *Standard Action, Instant, Varies, 15 Focus*. Prerequisites: Elemental Grab, Rank 4. *(Ego check vs. Resilience Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: The target can also be pinned and suffers the element's special effect (or is choked instead if the element doesn't suit suffocation).)* The character tightens their grip on an already-grabbed foe, making an Ego attack against the target's Resilience defense to deal damage and threaten suffocation.
- **Supernova** — *Standard Action, Instant, 15 Focus (scales: +1 Damage Modifier per 2 extra Focus, min 15 to activate)*. Prerequisites: Elemental Blast, Rank 4. *(Ego check vs. Resilience Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: affected targets take full damage and suffer that element's special effect.)* The character unleashes a wide elemental burst, checking Ego against the Resilience defense of every enemy within 10 spaces; extra spent Focus boosts the damage. A success deals half that damage, and a Fantastic result deals the full amount.

### Illusion (14)

- **Animated Illusion** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Static Illusion, Rank 2. Conjures a visual-only illusion anywhere within line of sight and 50 spaces, up to four sizes larger than the character, which can move on its own; concentration ends if the caster strays too far or loses sight of it.
- **Darkness** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Illumination, Rank 2. The character fills a wide area, anywhere within 50 spaces, with magical darkness that blocks all vision and douses non-power light sources, sustained by concentration.
- **Dazzle** — *Standard Action, Instant, 20 spaces, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Illumination, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Vigilance Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: Also deals regular damage in addition to blinding.)* The character blasts a foe in their line of sight with a blinding flash (or darkness) via an Ego check against Vigilance defense, blinding them for a turn.
- **Deafen** — *Standard Action, Instant, 20 spaces, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Illumination, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Vigilance Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: Also deals regular damage.)* The character makes an Ego check against a foe's Vigilance defense to deafen them for a turn.
- **Extend Invisibility** — *Standard Action, Concentration, Melee, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Invisibility. The character extends their invisibility to hide one other person or object within reach, able to cover a larger target as their rank increases.
- **Flare** — *Standard Action, Instant, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Illumination, Rank 3. *(Ego check vs. Vigilance Defense; Fantastic: The flare also deals regular damage to each enemy it blinds.)* The character unleashes a blinding burst of light, making an Ego check against the Vigilance defense of every nearby enemy to blind each one for a turn.
- **Grand Illusion** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Animated Illusion, Rank 3. The character conjures a realistic sight-and-sound illusion anywhere within line of sight and 50 spaces, up to five sizes larger than themselves, moving it freely as long as they keep sight of it and stay in range.
- **Group Invisibility** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 15 Focus*. Prerequisites: Extend Invisibility, Rank 4. For every point of Ego defense the character has, they can turn one additional person or object invisible along with themselves, each up to one size category larger per rank.
- **Illumination** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 0 Focus*. The character conjures bright light on a point or object they can see within 50 spaces, and can keep sustaining it even after moving out of range or losing sight of it.
- **Invisibility** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Rank 2. The character turns invisible, gaining an edge on Agility checks to sneak past others while enemies have trouble on Vigilance checks to spot them; a motionless invisible character can only be noticed if someone actively tries with a Vigilance check.
- **Mirror Images** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Animated Illusion, Rank 3. The character conjures one illusory duplicate of themselves per rank that looks, sounds, and moves independently, and can be swapped with; a duplicate that is struck vanishes, and the whole illusion ends the moment the real character attacks or otherwise gives themselves away.
- **Silence Area** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Illumination, Rank 2. The character conjures a zone of total silence (sized by rank) anywhere within 50 spaces, blocking sound from crossing in or out, and must stay within range of it to keep concentrating.
- **Silence Self** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Illumination, Rank 2. The character can silence themselves completely at will, gaining an edge on Agility checks to sneak and giving enemies trouble on Vigilance checks to notice them.
- **Static Illusion** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Illumination. The character conjures a purely visual, unmoving illusion anywhere within line of sight up to 50 spaces away, as large as three sizes bigger than themselves; losing sight of it or moving too far away ends the effect.

### Magic (25)

- **Bolts of Balthakk** — *Standard Action, Instant, 20 spaces, 5 Focus (scales: +1 Damage Modifier per 2 extra Focus, min 5 to activate)*. Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: Deals double damage and stuns the target for one round.)* Blasts a visible target's Agility defense with an Ego check that has an edge; the damage bonus scales with the Focus spent, and a Fantastic hit deals double damage and stuns the target for a round.

Prerequisites: Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Vigilance Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: Heals the character's full Focus loss instead of half.)* While holding a grabbed target, makes an Ego attack against their Vigilance to siphon Focus, healing half the Focus damage dealt back to the character.

Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 3. *(Ego check vs. Melee Defense; Fantastic: The target can also be pinned.)* The character binds a target in line of sight with magical crimson bands via an Ego check (with an edge) against Melee defense, paralyzing them; escaping requires a Melee check against target number 20.
- **Dispel Spell** — *Standard Action, Instant, 15 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 4. *(Ego check vs. Ego Defense; Fantastic: the target's concentration is broken entirely.)* The character makes an Ego check against a target's Ego defense to break their concentration on a power that requires it; a Fantastic success ends all of the target's concentration effects.
- **Exorcism** — *Standard Action, Instant, Melee, 15 Focus*. Prerequisites: Rank 4. *(Ego check vs. Ego Defense; Fantastic: All of the possessor's active concentration effects break, and the possessor is stunned for one round.)* The character makes an Ego check against a possessing entity's Ego defense to break its hold and force it out of the possessed target.
- **Flames of the Faltine** — *Standard Action, Instant, 10 spaces, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: The target takes double damage and then burns for 5 more damage at the end of each round until someone extinguishes the flames.)* The character hurls enchanted fire from their hands, making an Ego check against a visible target's Agility defense to burn them.
- **Hellfire Chains** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Cursed, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Resilience Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: The target is also grabbed and paralyzed by the chains.)* The character conjures hellfire chains to strike any target in line of sight with an Ego attack, dealing Health damage on a success and grabbing and paralyzing the target on a Fantastic success; a grabbed target can be attacked again each round for both Health and Focus damage, and can only break free with a Melee check against a target number of 20.
- **Hex Bolt** — *Standard Action, Instant, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Chaotic, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: Double damage and the target has trouble for one round.)* The character hurls a bolt of hexing magic, making an Ego check against the target's Agility defense; a success deals regular damage, and a Fantastic success deals double damage and leaves the target with trouble for one round.
- **Icy Tendrils of Ikthalon** — *Standard Action, Instant, 10 spaces, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Agility Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: Double damage and the target is frozen in place (paralyzed).)* The character lashes a target in line of sight with freezing tendrils, making an Ego check against their Agility defense; a success deals regular damage, and a Fantastic success doubles the damage and paralyzes the target in ice.
- **Images of Ikonn** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 2. The character conjures one fully controlled illusory duplicate per rank of a person they know (possibly themselves), each looking and sounding real; duplicates can move up to 10 spaces from the character and feint attacks, any duplicate that's actually struck vanishes instantly, and the whole illusion collapses if the character gives themselves away, such as by attacking for real. The same trick can also be used to see through someone else's illusions.
- **Jinx** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Chaotic, Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Ego Defense; Fantastic: The target also loses their next standard action.)* The character curses a target with an Ego check against their Ego defense; a success gives the target trouble on all their actions, and a Fantastic success also strips away their next standard action.
- **Leech Life** — *Reaction, Instant, Melee, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Rank 2. *(Ego check vs. Resilience Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: the character heals the full amount of damage dealt instead of half.)* As a reaction against a grabbed target, the character drains their life force with an Ego attack against their Resilience defense, dealing Health damage and healing half that amount themselves.
- **Mists of Morpheus** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 3. *(Ego check vs. Vigilance Defense; Fantastic: the target falls asleep instead of merely being stunned.)* The character makes an Ego check against a target's Vigilance defense to stun them for as long as the character concentrates.
- **Mists of Munnopor** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 5 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 2. While concentrating, the character conjures a large fog bank around themselves that blocks sight past 10 spaces and grounds anyone caught inside it, preventing flight, gliding, or web-slinging.
- **Penance Stare** — *Standard Action, Instant, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Cursed. *(Ego check vs. Ego Defense; deals damage; Fantastic: The target takes double damage and is paralyzed for one round.)* The character makes an Ego attack against a target within range; a success burns away their Focus (halved against Heroic-tagged targets), and if the target is left with none, their fate afterward is up to the Narrator.
- **Possess Vehicle** — *Standard Action, Concentration, Melee, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Cursed, Rank 3. The character magically seizes a vehicle they're in or touching, wreathing it in harmless hellfire that burns anyone who touches it, and pilots it at double speed with an edge on Ego-based operation checks, even up walls or through jumps, from up to 20 spaces per rank away.
- **Possession** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 20 Focus*. Prerequisites: Cursed, Rank 5. *(Ego check vs. Ego Defense.)* Against a Focus-less target, the character makes an Ego check with trouble versus the target's Ego defense to fully take over their body; the target can try to break free with an Ego check against the character's Ego defense, gaining an edge (or double edge, if Heroic) whenever the possessed body or the target themselves is used to cause harm.
- **Powerful Hex** — *Standard Action, Varies, 5 Focus (scales: +1 Damage Modifier per 2 extra Focus, min 5 to activate)*. Prerequisites: Chaotic, Rank 2. The character hexes themselves to use any other power they have the rank for, ignoring its usual prerequisites, paying that power's normal cost plus 5 extra Focus (or, for a numbered permanent power, 5 Focus per point); a copied permanent power runs on concentration instead.
- **Probability-Manipulation Hex** — *Standard Action, Concentration, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Chaotic, Rank 3. Whenever a nearby ally would have trouble on a check, this hex cancels the trouble and grants them an edge instead, for as long as the character concentrates.
- **Protection Hex** — *Standard Action, Concentration, Melee, 10 Focus*. Prerequisites: Chaotic, Rank 3. The character casts a protective hex, making an Ego check whose result becomes their defense against any attack for the duration (restartable if unsatisfied); a Fantastic result also grants Health Damage Reduction 1 while it lasts.
- **Sense Sins** — *Standard Action, Instant, Melee, 0 Focus*. Prerequisites: Cursed. *(Ego check vs. Ego Defense; Fantastic: The character also senses wrongdoing the target intends to commit in the near future.)* The character locks eyes with a target and rolls an Ego check against the target's Ego defense; success reveals wrongdoing the target has already committed.

- **Summon Portal** — *Standard Action, Concentration, Melee, 15 Focus*. Prerequisites: Sorcerous, Rank 4. ## Part 2 — Gear (Reference): Common Weapons (12)

Mundane weapons available to any character regardless of Power Set, sourced from the Marvel Multiverse RPG Core Rule Book's Common Weapons table (facts exact, wording original). Each is a `gear` Item with a working `attack` block, so it rolls exactly like a Power's attack — a melee or ranged check against the target's Resilience Defense — and (except grenades) adds a flat bonus to the wielder's damage multiplier for that attack only; this bonus does not stack with any other damage-multiplier bonus the character has, so only the higher of the two applies. None of the twelve grant any passive bonus, and none have a gold/Karma cost — they're just equipment.

- **Bow** — *Ranged 15, Agility attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +1 to the wielder's Agility damage multiplier when firing.
- **Club** — *Melee (Reach), Melee attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +1 to the wielder's Melee damage multiplier.
- **Flash-Bang Grenade** — *Ranged 10, Agility attack vs. Vigilance Defense; Fantastic: target also knocked prone.* Single-use thrown device; deals no Health damage and grants no damage-multiplier bonus. A hit leaves the target dazed and disoriented until the start of its next turn.
- **Frag Grenade** — *Ranged 10, Agility attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Single-use thrown explosive. Unlike the other Common Weapons its bonus isn't an addition to the damage multiplier — it doubles the wielder's normal damage multiplier for the throw instead.
- **Knife** — *Melee (Reach), Melee attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +1 to the wielder's Melee damage multiplier. Can also be thrown — see Knife, Thrown.
- **Knife, Thrown** — *Ranged 5, Agility attack vs. Resilience Defense.* The same knife, thrown instead of swung; adds +1 to the wielder's Agility damage multiplier when thrown.
- **Pistol** — *Ranged 10, Agility attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +1 to the wielder's Agility damage multiplier when firing.
- **Rifle** — *Ranged 20, Agility attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +1 to the wielder's Agility damage multiplier when firing.
- **Shotgun** — *Ranged 6, Agility attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +1 to the wielder's Agility damage multiplier when firing.
- **Sniper Rifle** — *Ranged 40, Agility attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +1 to the wielder's Agility damage multiplier when firing; the longest range of any Common Weapon.
- **Submachine Gun** — *Ranged 10, Agility attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +1 to the wielder's Agility damage multiplier when firing.
- **Sword** — *Melee (Reach), Melee attack vs. Resilience Defense.* Adds +2 to the wielder's Melee damage multiplier — the largest bonus of any Common Weapon.

## Part 3 — Traits (Reference): Core Rulebook Guidance

Unlike Powers or Gear, the book gives Traits no fixed catalog to compile — each character's Traits are written by that character's own player, tied to their specific backstory (p.21, p.63+). This section is the book's guidance rather than an itemized list.

**Traits vs. Tags.** Traits are mechanical: each one grants a concrete, describable benefit — almost always Edge or Trouble in a specific situation — and is tracked as its own item so it applies automatically, or as a one-click reminder, when that situation comes up. Tags are purely narrative labels (Rich, Secret Identity, Famous, Backup, Obligation, and the like) with no mechanical effect of their own.

**Where Traits come from.** Your Origin and Occupation (Step 3 of character creation) each come with a small starter package of Traits and Tags that people who share that background typically pick up. Beyond that starter package, your Rank determines how many additional Traits you can freely choose: you gain one extra Trait per Rank, so a Rank 1 character has 1 free pick and a Rank 6 character has 6.

**Common trigger patterns:**

- **Edge** — a specific, narrow situation where the character has a clear advantage.
- **Trouble** — the mirror case: a specific situation where the character is at a disadvantage.
- **Edge/Trouble pair** — one Trait carrying both a strength and a weakness that stem from the same piece of character.
- **Karma hook** — a Trouble trigger written so the GM has a clean cue to award Karma when it actually costs the character something at the table.
- **One-shot ability** — a small number of Traits grant a once-per-scene action instead of a roll modifier.

**Writing your own.** A Trait works best when it's specific enough that the table can immediately picture the moment it applies, tied to one concrete detail from the character's actual backstory rather than a generic archetype. See Part 5 below for 24 worked examples from the Wickfield Eight, and Part 6 for two more from their adversaries.
